## Decisions taken
- **Cancelled orders:** cancelling gives back stock exactly once. The discount use stays consumed. A cancelled order can't be reopened.
- **Stock on product edits:** a product save writes stock only when the stock field was changed, and returns 409 if the stock moved in the meantime.
- **Tax rates:** a tax rate that is in use can't be deleted. One failing recurring schedule no longer stops reminders.
- **Invoice overpayment:** an overpaid invoice records the payment, keeps the balance at 0 and shows "Overpaid by X" to the business.
- **BookLink phone:** a phone number is required on bookings, because customers are keyed by phone.
- **Short-let extra costs:** these are shown as "paid to the host, not charged here".
- **Plan billing copy:** billing says "Ends {date}" or "Complimentary until {date}" in every app, because nothing renews automatically.
- **Featured boosts:** they show first on the PropertyLink home page, from any city.
- **Current plan:** one rule applies everywhere. Among subscriptions in force, the highest plan wins, then the longest-running one. A comp of a lower plan never overrides a paid plan; this restores the earlier deliberate fix.
